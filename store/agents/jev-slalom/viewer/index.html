How it works

        

          Every tick Jev gets the lines above, the skier, the next gate and how many
          rows away it is, and answers one typed question: steer LEFT,
          RIGHT, a _FAST version of either, or HOLD. Pass
          between the poles or the run is over.


          The skier moves at most 1.9 slots sideways a tick. Raise speed and there are
          fewer ticks between gates, until the skier cannot cross the valley in time. The course and
          the wind are made up. The decision loop is the demo.
