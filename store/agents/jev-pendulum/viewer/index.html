How it works

        

          Jev never sees pixels. About twelve times a second the rig is written as the text above,
          and Jev answers two typed questions in one call: which way to push the cart,
          and whether the rod is still under control. The five arrows on the cart are its
          options, sized by probability. The green one is the push it made.


          Gravity pulls harder the more the rod leans. The hardest shove is fixed. Past the
          no return lines gravity wins, whatever Jev answers. Raise gravity or shorten the rod
          and that limit comes sooner. The rig is made up. The decision loop is the demo.
