How it works

        

          Jev never sees the picture. Every move, the board is written as the text above and Jev
          answers four typed questions in one call: which way to move, what it is
          heading for, whether it is sure, and whether it is boxed in.
          The arrows on the board are its answer, sized by probability.


          Jev only knows the walls it has seen. The step counts treat dark floor as open, so with a
          short sight the dotted way runs through walls it has not met, and it has to back
          out of dead ends.  The world is made up. Edit
          arena.json to draw your own.
