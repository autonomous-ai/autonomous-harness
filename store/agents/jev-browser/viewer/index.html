How it works

        

          Jev never sees the screen. Each step the page is written out as the text above, and Jev
          picks one element out of the list, a new list every step, plus two yes/no reads, in one
          call. Clicking an input fills it with the right value from the task.


          Distraction adds cookie walls, pop-ups, decoy buttons and layout shifts that land
          between reading the page and clicking it, so a click can hit the wrong thing. Every
          booking is checked against the task. The site and the flights are made up.
