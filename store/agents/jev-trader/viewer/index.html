How it works

        

          Jev never sees the chart. Each day the last 20 closing prices and the account are written as
          the text above, and Jev answers five typed questions in one call: buy, hold or sell,
          how strong the signal is, whether it is a clear call, what the trend is, and whether a
          crash is under way. The three bars on the newest candle are Jev's probabilities.


          The coloured ribbon under the chart is the hidden trend that drives the made-up
          price. Jev cannot see it. With little noise it reads the trend from the tape and beats
          buy-and-hold. With a lot of noise it is a coin flip. Edit market.json to
          reshape the market. No real asset, no real money, not financial advice.
