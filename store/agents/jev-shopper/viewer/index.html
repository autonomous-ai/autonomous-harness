    How it works
    
        
    
          The shop is made up. Every product has a hidden fair price that now and then goes on a
          real sale. The price on the board is the fair price plus noise. On every tick Jev
          reads the board as the text above and answers two typed questions in one call: which
          product is the best buy right now, and whether to spend now.

    
          The spotlight is Jev's answer. The fainter beams and the bar on each card are the
          probability it gave every product. When Jev says spend and its pick is under the deal bar,
          an order goes in and lands one tick later. A real sale is still there. A wobble is
          gone, so noise turns savings into nothing.

    
          Edit shopper.json to change the products, the noise or the budget. None of
          this is shopping or money advice.
