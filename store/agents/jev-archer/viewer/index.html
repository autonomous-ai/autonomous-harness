How it works

        

          Every tick Jev gets the two numbers above, the aim and the target, and answers
          one typed question: nudge the aim LEFT, RIGHT or HOLD.
          Every sixth tick the arrow goes where the aim is. On the gold it is a bullseye.


          The aim moves 0.8 slots a tick. Push speed past that and the target outruns
          it. The range, the target and the wind are made up. The decision loop is the demo.
