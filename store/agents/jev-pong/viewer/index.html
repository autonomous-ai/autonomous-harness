How it works

        

          Jev never sees pixels. About sixteen times a second the court is written as the text
          above, and Jev answers two typed questions in one call: which paddle move to
          make, and whether it will reach the ball. The faint paddles are its five options,
          lit by probability. The ring on its wall is where the ball will cross.


          Every return makes the ball faster. The paddle never gets faster. Once the ball is past
          the red mark on the pace meter, a ball at the far end of the wall cannot be reached, and
          the rally ends there. The court is made up. The decision loop is the demo.
