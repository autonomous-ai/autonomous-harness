How it works

        

          Jev never sees pixels. About nine times a second the marine's situation is written as the
          text above, and Jev answers four typed questions in one call: where to turn, how
          to move, whether to fire, and how big the threat is. The keys lit on screen
          are Jev's answers, filled by their probability.


          Each cleared wave adds a demon and makes them faster, until Jev's decision rate is not
          enough. Edit level.json to redraw the map or change the pace. The arena and
          the demons are made up. The decision loop is the demo.
