How it works

        

          Every tick Jev gets the line above, the glove and where the next ball lands,
          and answers one typed question: slide LEFT, RIGHT, a
          _FAST version of either, or HOLD. The ball is an out only if the
          glove is within reach of the spot when it comes down.


          The glove covers at most 1.2 slots a tick. Cut fallTicks and a far ball
          lands before the glove can get there. The park, the flies and the wind are made up. The
          decision loop is the demo.
