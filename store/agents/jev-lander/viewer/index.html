How it works

        

          Jev never sees pixels. Every tick the telemetry is written as the text above, and Jev
          answers two typed questions in one call: which throttle to set, and whether
          the touchdown will be soft. The throttle gauge on the right of the scene shows
          all four options, filled by probability.


          Full BURN pushes 2.6. Gravity pulls all the time, and every tick in the air burns fuel.
          The bar under the booster is the height it needs to stop at full burn. When it reaches
          past the pad, or the tank runs dry, no answer can save the landing. The flight is made
          up. The decision loop is the demo.
