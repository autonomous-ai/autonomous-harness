Jev, the flight computer
A booster is falling out of the sky. Every tick Jev reads altitude, vertical speed and fuel and sets a throttle: CUT (none), COAST (idle), HOVER (hold) or BURN (full). Touch down soft and it's a clean landing; hit hard and the booster is scrap.

Edit lander.json: crank gravity up and Jev's burns get twitchy and landings start to crash; shrink fuel and it has less room to recover.
