How it works one call per piece · the limit is time
One call per piece. Jev gets every legal placement and answers place, danger and go_for_four at once. The ghosts on the board are its probabilities. The limit is time: the piece keeps falling while Jev thinks and while it is moved, one step at a time. Nothing is random. The game is made up.
