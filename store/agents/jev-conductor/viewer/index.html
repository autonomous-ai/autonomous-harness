How it works

      

        Jev writes the tune one bar at a time, two calls a bar. The first picks the
        chord, the mood and the energy. The second picks the bass and
        every lead note, one typed question per note, all in one call. The faint notes on the
        roll are the other notes Jev weighed, as strong as their probability.


        Memory is the honest dial. It is how many of its own last bars Jev can read. With a
        few bars it follows each chord with one that leads on, holds a mood for a phrase, and picks
        the melody up where it left it. At 0 it cannot. The tune is made up. Edit
        piece.json to change the scale, the chords and the moods.
