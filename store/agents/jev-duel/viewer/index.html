    How it works
    
      
    
        Jev plays both sides and a third Jev is the referee. Each player reads the board
        and its list of legal moves as text and answers with a probability for every move. Those
        are the dots on the board. After each move the referee scores it: how strong, how
        aggressive, how decided the game is.

    
        Reads is the honest dial. At 0 a player is told only how many disks each move flips.
        At 1 it is also told where the square sits. At 2 it is also told what its rival can do in
        reply. The game is made up. Edit battle.json to change the rivals.
